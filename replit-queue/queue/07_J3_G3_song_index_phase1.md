NETWORK BUDGET AND MANNERS (hard limits):
- Only stream/status URLs that come from liveRadioDirectory.json / liveRadioSources.json. Every hop, including redirects, goes through the existing isUrlSafe / redirect validation.
- At most 1 request per host per 5 s; ICY probes read at most metaint + 4 KB and abort at 10 s; no more than 2 connections open at once overall.
- Total budget for steps 2-3: 600 requests. Step 4 capture run: 12 stations, 1 poll per 30 s for 60 minutes (about 1,440 polls). Report exact counts used.
- On HTTP 403/429 from a host: stop probing that host, record it, do not retry, do not rotate IPs or headers to evade.
- Run probe and capture scripts with `NET_GUARD=count NET_GUARD_BUDGET_HOSTS=archive.org NET_GUARD_LOG=docs/song-index-phase1/raw/net-guard.ndjson node --import ./tools/net-guard.mjs <script>`: COUNT mode lets the station requests through and counts them per host, and the `watched(archive.org)` figure in the guard line must be 0.
