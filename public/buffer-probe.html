AJN buffer probe

Diagnostic page. Read-only: it only requests media through your own proxy and measures. Put it in public/, open /buffer-probe.html on the live app (same origin as the proxy). Nothing here is simulated: every number comes from the browser or from response headers.

1. Items (one per line: group | label | /download/identifier/file.mp4)

Path may be an archive path (it is wrapped as /api/archive/proxy?path=…) or a full URL starting with /api/. Groups used for the summary: movies, tv, audio, other.

5. How to read it

bitrate b (kbps)  = 8 * totalBytes / durationSeconds / 1000        (totalBytes from Content-Range, duration from the video element)
8 MiB slice (s)   = 8*1024*1024*8 / (b*1000)                          (how many seconds one proxy slice holds)
delivery rate     = bytes received by fetch / seconds (after first byte)
ρ                 = delivery rate / (b*1000/8)                         ρ≈1: proxy/upstream hands data out at about the playback rate.  ρ≥3: data arrives fast.
fill x            = (bufferedEnd gain) / (wall seconds) over the steady window     ≈1: buffer grows at playback speed only
verdicts: HEALTHY (median ahead ≥ 30 s) | UPSTREAM_PACED (ahead < 10 s and ρ < 1.5) | CLIENT_OR_SLICE_LIMITED (ahead < 10 s and ρ ≥ 3) | MIXED (otherwise) | NO_DELIVERY_DATA (arm F off).
